## Minimum review rules

- Preserve the source page range and content version.
- Do not add facts not supported by the source passage.
- For calculations, record the verified answer separately from the explanation.
- Flag pages where illustrations supply essential meaning; text extraction alone is insufficient.
- Use simple English appropriate for Grade 3.
